Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

We currently ship orders within India. Please review the information below before placing an order.

Delivery Timelines

Shipping MethodEstimated Delivery Time
Surface5–7 business days
Air2–3 business days

Custom prints, models, and made-to-order products require an additional 1–2 business days for production before shipping. Production time is added automatically to the estimated delivery timeline.

For example, a custom order shipped by Surface may generally take 6–9 business days in total, while an order shipped by Air may generally take 3–5 business days.

Delivery estimates are calculated in business days and begin once your order has been printed, packed, and handed over to the shipping carrier.

We will provide order-status updates by email at relevant stages of processing and shipping.

Shipping Rates

Shipping charges are calculated based on the shipping method and delivery destination selected at checkout.

The final shipping cost may vary depending on the actual shipping requirements and destination. If the amount charged for shipping is higher than the actual applicable shipping cost, the excess amount will be refunded after delivery.

Approved shipping-cost refunds are processed through our payment gateway and may take up to 7 business days to reflect in the original payment method, depending on the payment method and the customer's bank or financial institution.

Where applicable, a shipping receipt or shipping-related documentation will be provided along with the order.

Order Tracking

You will receive a confirmation email containing tracking details once your order has been handed over to the shipping carrier.

You can also track your order at:

Track Your Order

Tracking information may take some time to become active after the package has been handed over to the carrier.

Delays

The delivery timelines provided above are estimates and not guaranteed delivery dates. Delivery may occasionally take longer due to carrier delays, adverse weather, high-demand periods, public holidays, transportation disruptions, or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

We will monitor the status of your shipment and notify you by email if we become aware of a significant delay.

Delays caused by an incorrect or incomplete delivery address, failure to receive the shipment, or other circumstances attributable to the customer may require additional delivery attempts or charges and may affect the estimated delivery time.

Shipping Carrier

Orders are handed over to third-party shipping and logistics partners, which may include carriers such as Blue Dart, Delhivery, India Post, or other suitable shipping providers.

The carrier used may vary depending on the destination, shipping method, availability, and operational requirements.

Once an order has been handed over to the carrier, tracking and delivery are carried out through the carrier's logistics network. However, if you experience a delivery issue, you may contact Snap Print at queries@snaprint.in, and we will assist with the shipment where reasonably possible.

Where We Ship

We currently ship to addresses within India only.

Please ensure that your shipping address, phone number, and other delivery details provided at checkout are accurate and complete. Snap Print cannot be responsible for delays or failed deliveries resulting from incorrect or incomplete information provided by the customer.

Questions

For any shipping-related questions or concerns, please email queries@snaprint.in with your order number and relevant details.
