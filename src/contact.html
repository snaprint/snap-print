For Order Issues

If you're contacting us about a damaged, defective, incorrect, or materially different product, please include:

  • Your order number
  • A brief description of the issue
  • Clear photographs and/or video showing the issue
  • An unboxing video for transit-damage claims, where available

Please report damaged, defective, or incorrect orders within 24 hours of delivery as specified in our Refund & Return Policy.

Custom Parts & Engineering Requests

For custom engineering parts, please email us with as much information as possible:

  • Description of the part and intended application
  • Dimensions and required tolerances
  • Quantity needed
  • Material requirements, if applicable
  • CAD or 3D model files
  • Reference drawings or images

Providing complete information helps us determine whether your requirement is suitable for our manufacturing process.
