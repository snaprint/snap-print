Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, orders, shipping, payments, and more.

General

Snap Print is a 3D-printing business based in Vadodara, Gujarat, India. We offer 3D-printed products including decor, toys, accessories, models, and custom engineering parts.

Our products are currently manufactured using PLA (Polylactic Acid) filament.

PLA is suitable for many everyday and decorative applications, but it has temperature and mechanical limitations. Unless specifically stated otherwise, our products should not be considered safety-certified, load-bearing, or suitable for critical applications.

Product images are representative. Because 3D printing is a physical manufacturing process, minor variations in colour, texture, layer appearance, dimensions, or finish may occur.

Minor variations that do not materially affect the product's functionality, specifications, or appearance are considered normal characteristics of 3D printing.

Products that require additional production time will be identified accordingly. Custom prints, models, and made-to-order products generally require an additional 1–2 business days for production before shipping.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges depend on the shipping method and delivery destination selected at checkout.

If the amount charged for shipping exceeds the actual applicable shipping cost, the excess amount will be refunded after delivery.

Approved shipping-cost refunds may take up to 7 business days to reflect in the original payment method, depending on the payment method and the customer's bank or financial institution.

Returns, Refunds & Damaged Orders

Generally, no. Because many Snap Print products are custom-made or produced specifically after an order is placed, we do not accept returns or provide refunds solely because a customer has changed their mind after placing an order.

This does not affect any consumer rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded.

Please contact us at queries@snaprint.in within 24 hours of delivery.

For transit damage, we strongly require a clear, continuous unboxing video showing the package in its delivered condition, including the shipping label, before opening and throughout the unboxing process.

Contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photographs and/or video showing the issue.

If the product is verified as defective, incorrect, or materially different from the agreed specifications, we will review the claim and provide an appropriate remedy in accordance with our Refund & Return Policy and applicable law.

Once a refund has been approved and initiated, it may take up to 7 business days to reflect in the original payment method, depending on the payment method and the customer's bank or financial institution.

You can request cancellation for a full refund if your request is received within 6 hours of payment confirmation and before production begins, whichever occurs first.

Once production or printing has started, cancellation due to a change of mind is no longer possible.

A cancellation is considered accepted only after Snap Print confirms it.

Custom Engineering Parts

Yes. Snap Print can manufacture suitable custom parts based on customer-provided designs and specifications.

The feasibility of a custom part depends on factors such as geometry, dimensions, material requirements, tolerances, intended application, and manufacturing limitations.

Custom parts can be manufactured from customer-provided designs where the file and requirements are suitable for production.

Please provide the relevant design file, dimensions, tolerances, material requirements, and any other information necessary to manufacture the part correctly.

Custom and made-to-order parts are generally not returnable or refundable due to a change of mind, incorrect customer requirements, or reasons unrelated to a defect or error on our part.

If a custom part is defective, damaged, incorrect, or does not conform to the specifications agreed upon at the time of purchase, contact us within 24 hours of delivery so that we can review the issue.
